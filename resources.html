Protect your shipment

CARGO PREPARATION ESSENTIALS

01

Pack for the journey

Use suitable outer packaging, fill empty space and add clear labels on every handling unit. Fragile items need internal protection and visible handling marks.

02

Measure accurately

Confirm packed dimensions, total weight and pallet count before booking. Accurate details help us select safe equipment and avoid delays or recharges.

03

Prepare documents

Keep commercial invoices, packing lists and any permits ready. Tell us early about dangerous goods, temperature control or special handling requirements.

This guide is a starting point. Requirements can vary by route, cargo type and destination. Contact our team for shipment-specific advice.

Before You Book

SHIPPING DETAILS TO HAVE READY

Cargo Information

Product type, number of packages, packed dimensions, total weight and any special handling needs.

Route Information

Collection and delivery addresses, preferred dates and whether the shipment has a strict deadline.

Commercial Documents

Commercial invoice, packing list and any certificates or permits required for the cargo or destination.

Helpful Reminders

KEEP YOUR CARGO MOVING

Book early

Earlier planning gives you more carrier choices and protects critical delivery dates.

Label every package

Use consistent shipping marks so cargo stays identifiable at every transfer point.

Declare accurately

Clear, complete commodity information supports smooth customs processing.

Insure important goods

Ask about cargo insurance when the value or risk profile calls for extra protection.
